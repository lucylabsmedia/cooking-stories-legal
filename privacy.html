Privacy Policy

Cooking Stories: A Word Search Recipe Game

Effective Date: September 26, 2026 | Application ID: com.brainfit.cookingstories

1. Overview & Commitment to Privacy

We believe players deserve an uncluttered, peaceful culinary experience. Cooking Stories is designed with privacy by design:

  • No Account Creation: You do not need to create an account, log in, or provide personal credentials to play.
  • 100% Ad-Free: We do not include third-party advertising SDKs, ad networks, tracking pixels, or cross-app behavioral trackers.
  • Offline Playability: Your puzzle progression and unlocked recipe box items are stored locally on your device.

2. Information We Process & Third-Party Service Providers

To support optional in-app purchases and verify game licenses, we utilize industry-standard, secure infrastructure:

  • Google Play In-App Billing: All financial transactions, credit card handling, and billing information are collected and processed directly by Google Play. We never receive or store your payment details.
  • RevenueCat: We utilize RevenueCat to validate digital receipt tokens and manage in-app entitlements (e.g., verifying your lifetime unlock status across app reinstalls). RevenueCat processes anonymous app user identifiers and purchase receipt timestamps strictly to fulfill your purchased access.

3. Device Permissions & File Access

Cooking Stories utilizes standard Android system features solely to support gameplay and recipe utilities:

  • PDF Recipe Export & Printing: When you choose to export or print an heirloom recipe card, the app invokes the native Android Print Spooler. The app does not inspect, read, or upload personal files from your device storage.

4. Beta Testing Feedback & Intake

If you participate in our optional Closed Beta program and submit feedback via our testing intake forms, any provided information (such as your tester email address, device model, and bug descriptions) is used strictly to diagnose crashes, resolve glitches, and improve game stability prior to public release. We never sell, rent, or monetize tester information.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA & GDPR-K Compliance)

Cooking Stories is a family-friendly word search and baking puzzle game intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 (or under 16 in the European Union). Because the game features no social networking, public chat, or third-party behavioral ads, children can enjoy solving puzzles without exposure to tracking.

6. Data Retention & Deletion

Because core game progression is kept on your device, deleting or uninstalling the app permanently removes local progress data. For any questions regarding receipt validation records handled by Google Play or RevenueCat, you may contact us directly to request data verification or deletion.

7. Policy Changes

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect new features or platform requirements. Any updates will be published to this URL with a revised effective date.
