Privacy Policy

Cooking Stories: A Word Search Recipe Game
Last Updated: August 2026

Lucy Labs Media ("we," "our," or "us") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how data is handled when you use Cooking Stories: A Word Search Recipe Game (the "App"). We are committed to minimizing data collection and ensuring a secure, offline-first casual gaming experience.

1. Information We Collect and Storage

  • Local Game Progress & Preferences: Your game level unlocks, recipe collection, active streak, audio settings, and reminder preferences are stored strictly on your local device using Android DataStore / SharedPreferences. This data is not uploaded to our servers.
  • In-App Purchases: All billing and payments are securely handled by the Google Play Store and RevenueCat. We never collect or store your payment details.
  • Local Notifications: The Daily Morning Word Search Reminder is scheduled locally on your device. No personal identifier or token is sent to external servers.
  • Tester Feedback: Information submitted voluntarily via our Google Form (such as email, device model, and bug notes) is used exclusively to troubleshoot bugs.

2. Permissions

The App requests POST_NOTIFICATIONS on Android 13+ solely to deliver your opt-in morning baking reminder. You can toggle this off anytime in Settings.

3. Children’s Privacy

The App does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13.
