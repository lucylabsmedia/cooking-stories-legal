Privacy Policy

Cooking Stories: A Word Search Recipe Game
Last Updated: September 2026

Lucy Labs Media ("we," "our," or "us") respects your personal privacy. This Privacy Policy explains our data practices for Cooking Stories: A Word Search Recipe Game (the "App"). We hold an offline-first, zero-tracking philosophy: the App is engineered to deliver a serene culinary puzzle experience without harvesting your personal information, tracking your web behavior, or serving targeted advertisements.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

To ensure complete peace of mind, we explicitly disclose that:

  • We do not require an account, email address, or social login to play.
  • We do not collect or store sensitive personal data (including health conditions, dietary restrictions, or medical history).
  • We do not integrate third-party advertising SDKs, data brokers, or behavioral tracking networks.
  • We do not access your device’s camera, microphone, contacts, location, or photo gallery.

2. Information Stored Locally on Your Device

  • Gameplay Progress & Unlocked Recipes: Your completed levels, chapter stars, unlocked recipe cards, active playing streak, sound/music toggles, and audio volume are stored 100% locally on your device hardware using secure Android Jetpack DataStore / Room database storage. This data is never transmitted to our servers.
  • Local PDF Generation & Kitchen Printing: When you export a recipe card or the Collector's Cookbook to PDF, the document is assembled entirely on-device and passed directly to your native Android Print Spooler. We have zero visibility into what documents you print or save to your local file storage.
  • Native Sharing: If you use the optional recipe share action, the share payload is handed off directly to your Android operating system's native share sheet. No record of your contacts or outgoing messages is ever gathered or stored by the App.

3. Third-Party Service Providers (Purchases & Troubleshooting)

The App relies on trusted, standard mobile operating system services for payment processing and optional feedback:

  • Google Play Billing & RevenueCat: Optional in-app purchases (such as the Full Game Unlock or Master Chef Pass) are processed directly by Google Play In-App Billing and validated via RevenueCat. We never receive, process, or store your credit card details, banking credentials, or billing address. Transactions are governed by Google's Privacy Policy.
  • Voluntary Beta Feedback: If you choose to submit feedback or report a software bug via our voluntary Google Forms portal, the information you provide (such as your email address, device model, and bug notes) is used exclusively by our engineering team to resolve bugs and improve game stability. It is never sold or used for marketing outreach.

4. Device Permissions

  • POST_NOTIFICATIONS (Android 13+): Requested solely if you opt in to receive a gentle daily morning baking reminder. These notifications are scheduled and triggered 100% locally by your device’s internal clock. No push token or device identifier is transmitted to any cloud notification server. You can disable notifications anytime in the in-game settings or your device’s system settings.

5. Children’s Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

The App is a peaceful casual game intended for general audiences. We do not knowingly collect, solicit, or maintain personal information from children under 13 years of age (or under 16 in certain jurisdictions). Because the App does not collect personal data from any user, it operates in strict compliance with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) and international privacy frameworks.

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect game updates or legal requirements. Any modifications will be posted directly to this page with an updated revision date. Your continued use of the App following any update constitutes your acceptance of the revised policy.
